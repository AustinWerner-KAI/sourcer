# Sourcer Chrome button

Saves the LinkedIn profile you are viewing to a Sourcer role (SRS F10). It
reads only the open page, only when you click, and sends nothing until you
press Save. It never visits LinkedIn on its own.

## Install (once per Mac, about a minute)

1. In Chrome, open `chrome://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `sourcer/extension` folder
   (on the office Mac: `~/sourcer-pilot/sourcer/extension`).
4. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin **Sourcer**.
5. Right-click the Sourcer button, choose **Options**, enter the address you
   open Sourcer at, press Save, then **Allow**.

Sign in to Sourcer in the same Chrome. Then open a LinkedIn profile, click the
button, check the details, pick the role and press **Save to Sourcer**.

After a Sourcer update, click the reload arrow on the Sourcer card in
`chrome://extensions`.
